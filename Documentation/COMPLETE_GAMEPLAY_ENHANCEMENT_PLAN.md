## ⚔️ **3. Enemy & Monster System**

### **👹 Monster Types:**
```
Rank E Monsters (Beginner):
- Giant Ants, Ice Bears, Wolf Packs
- Basic AI, simple attack patterns
- Drop: Basic materials, low-tier items

Rank D Monsters (Intermediate):
- Orcs, Goblins, Lesser Demons
- Group AI, coordinated attacks
- Drop: Medium-tier materials, skill books

Rank C Monsters (Advanced):
- High Orcs, Demon Knights, Beast Lords
- Complex AI, special abilities
- Drop: High-tier materials, rare items

Rank B Monsters (Expert):
- Dragon Kin, Demon Generals, Ancient Beasts
- Strategic AI, environmental usage
- Drop: Epic materials, unique items

Rank A Monsters (Elite):
- Demon Lords, Ancient Dragons, Monarch Servants
- Boss-level AI, phase transitions
- Drop: Legendary materials, artifacts

Rank S Monsters (Legendary):
- Monarchs, Rulers, World Bosses
- Ultimate AI, reality-warping abilities
- Drop: Mythic materials, system upgrades
```

### **🧠 AI Behavior Systems:**
- **Pack AI** - Coordinate group attacks
- **Territorial AI** - Guard specific areas
- **Hunting AI** - Track and ambush players
- **Boss AI** - Multi-phase combat patterns
- **Learning AI** - Adapt to player strategies
