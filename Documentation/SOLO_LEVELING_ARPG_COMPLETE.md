## 🎮 Game Features

### 🌑 Core Gameplay
- **Hunter System**: 6 unique classes, E-Rank to National Level progression
- **Shadow Army**: Extract and command shadows from defeated enemies
- **Combat System**: Advanced combat with skills, combos, and special abilities
- **Dungeon System**: Procedurally generated dungeons with scaling difficulty
- **Item System**: Comprehensive inventory, equipment, and loot mechanics

### ⚡ Advanced Systems
- **AI System**: Neural network-based enemy AI with learning capabilities
- **Quantum Computing**: Quantum state simulation for complex calculations
